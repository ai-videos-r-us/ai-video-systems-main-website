## How I got here

**Exposure VC (2015–2017).** My first business: videography, corporate and social video, website design, SEO and lead generation for local businesses. It generated hundreds of thousands of pounds for clients — and taught me that content without a commercial system behind it is just noise.

**Solutions in Technology, then Onecom (2017–2020).** B2B telecom sales. Years of outbound calling, discovery conversations, qualification and objection handling for one of the UK's leading business telecoms providers. This is where I learned what a *qualified* opportunity actually is — and how much revenue dies between "lead" and "conversation."

**My Lead Machine (2020–2022).** I founded an appointment-setting agency for the mortgage industry. Brokers didn't need more leads; they needed better ones. We built a pre-qualification framework and delivered vetted appointments to dozens of brokers, who collectively closed millions of pounds in mortgage deals.

**Organic Prospecting Systems (2022–2024).** A B2B client-acquisition company combining LinkedIn, cold email, cold calling and conversion funnels. We generated thousands of qualified appointments — campaigns capable of 100+ appointments a month without paid ads — and I trained and managed the sales development team that ran them.

**Three Protocol and Block AI (2024–2025).** Go-to-market, positioning and community growth in Web3. At Three Protocol I led the marketing function as the project grew to a $48M market capitalisation, building a 15,000-member Telegram community. At Block AI I built an 8,500-subscriber newsletter and ran AI-powered outreach. This is where I saw first-hand what AI-assisted content could do at volume — and where most people using it were getting it wrong.

**AI Video Systems (2025–present).** Everything above, assembled into one system. Most businesses ask cold ads to do the entire sale at once: capture attention, explain the offer, build trust, handle objections and generate the enquiry. We move that work upstream — extracting a company's real sales intelligence and turning it into an attention-to-revenue system that creates demand before anyone is asked to buy.

## What I believe about marketing

These convictions run through every article on this blog:

1. **A lead is only valuable because of what happens next.** Lead volume without follow-up, qualification and close data is a vanity number.
2. **The sale starts before the landing page.** Conversion is mostly decided by what a prospect saw and believed before they clicked.
3. **Views measure distribution, not business.** The metric that matters is qualified sales conversations.
4. **Speed-to-lead is marketing performance, not admin.** The hard data on response time is brutal, and almost nobody acts on it.
5. **AI is leverage on a strategy — not the strategy.** AI video is infrastructure inside an acquisition system, not the product.
6. **Marketing and sales are one loop.** Most "marketing problems" are a leak in the middle of that loop.
