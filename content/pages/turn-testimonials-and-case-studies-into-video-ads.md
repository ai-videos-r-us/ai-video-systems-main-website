To turn testimonials and case studies into video ads, pick the ones that name a specific problem and a specific outcome, get written permission, and structure each ad as problem, what changed, result, using the customer's real words as the spine. Keep real footage and voices real, and use AI only for edits, captions and supporting visuals.

Proof is the closest thing an ad has to a referral, so it's worth handling carefully.

## Which testimonials make the best ads?

Choose for specificity, not enthusiasm. "Great service!" persuades nobody; "we'd been quoted for three weeks of disruption and the kitchen was done in eight days" does. Look for:

- A named problem the viewer shares
- A concrete outcome (time, cost, result, feeling)
- An objection answered (price, trust, disruption)
- A real person willing to be on record

## What structure works?

Three beats, short:

1. **The problem, in their words.** The moment the viewer recognises themselves.
2. **What changed.** The one thing that made the difference, in plain language.
3. **The result.** Specific and checkable. For a funeral-plan provider, the ad that won spoke to the real reason people buy, not wanting to leave a burden on family.

Then one clear next step.

## How do you turn a case study into a video?

Lead with the result, then the situation, then what changed. Use real numbers with what was measured and over what period. Our own case study is a model: in 30 days qualified opportunities rose from 24 to 60 a month on 29.5% less ad spend, and we published the sales numbers as still maturing rather than waiting for a cleaner story. [Read the full case study](/blog/compare-funerals-30-day-case-study). Honest limits build more trust than round numbers.

## What are the rules?

- **Written permission** that covers advertising use, per person.
- **Don't alter what they said,** and don't edit meaning.
- **Never generate a testimonial or a customer.** See [will AI video ads look fake?](/guides/will-ai-video-ads-look-fake-and-damage-my-brand).
- **Check regulated claims** against your industry's rules, and platform policies.

## How do you know it's working?

Compare ad sets by qualified calls and cost per customer, not views. Proof-led ads should move the numbers that referrals move: show rate, close rate and average value. See [how to measure real ROI](/guides/measure-real-roi-of-facebook-ads).
