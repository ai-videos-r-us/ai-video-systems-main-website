AI Video Systems has tracked $60M+ in revenue across 96+ clients. Three accounts are documented in detail: Compare Funerals (qualified funeral-plan opportunities up from 24 to 60 a month on 29.5% less ad spend in 30 days), Mortgage Fit (20+ qualified mortgage leads in five weeks) and Ironclad Finance (5M+ views and 74 enquiries in seven weeks). Each is below with what it does and doesn't prove.

## Compare Funerals: 30 days, funeral plans

**What it is:** a funeral-plan provider serving British expatriates in Spain, on Meta lead ads. We compared the seven months before we took over (1 January to 2 August 2026) with our first 32 days (3 August to 3 September 2026), both normalised to 30 days.

| Metric | Before (per 30 days) | After (per 30 days) | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Qualified leads | 24.1 | 60 | +148.8% |
| Cost per qualified lead | £521.07 | £147.65 | -71.7% |
| Meta cost per lead | £21.58 | £9.63 | -55.4% |
| Monthly ad spend | £12,564 | £8,859 | -29.5% |

**What it doesn't prove:** revenue. It's an acquisition-efficiency case study; the sales numbers were still maturing and we published them as such. "Qualified" means the client's own sales team marked the lead as a genuine prospect. [Read the full account](/blog/compare-funerals-30-day-case-study).

## Mortgage Fit: five weeks, mortgage leads

**What it is:** an AI-led content and lead-generation campaign for a mortgage business. It produced hundreds of thousands of targeted views and more than 20 qualified mortgage leads in five weeks, in a category where trust decides the sale before price comes up.

**What it doesn't prove:** how long the leads take to become clients, or that the same volume repeats in every category. See [mortgage broker lead generation](/mortgage-broker-lead-generation).

## Ironclad Finance: seven weeks, video and enquiries

**What it is:** an AI-led campaign for an established financial-services business: more than 5 million views and 74 inbound enquiries in seven weeks.

**What it doesn't prove:** that views cause enquiries on their own. The campaign worked because the business had a proven offer, real proof and a way to capture the interest; see [is AI video marketing worth it?](/blog/is-ai-video-marketing-worth-it) for the conditions. See also [video lead generation](/video-lead-generation).

## $60M+ across 96+ clients

**What it is:** the client revenue AI Video Systems has tracked across its client base. The free call is the place to ask how it's counted.

**What it doesn't prove:** what any single new client will get. Results depend on your offer, follow-up and proof.
