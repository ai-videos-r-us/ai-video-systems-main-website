Lead generation for a private clinic works when the ad builds trust in your clinicians, the form qualifies, reception responds in minutes, bookings are confirmed and reminded, and every enquiry is tracked to an attended consultation and a treatment, all within healthcare-advertising rules. We build and run that for established clinics already spending $5,000 or more a month on ads.

## Why do clinics lose enquiries?

1. **Slow response.** Across 5.7 million leads, conversion was 8 times higher when the first call came within five minutes ([the data](/blog/speed-to-lead-5-minute-rule)).
2. **No-shows.** A booking with no confirmation or reminder is a ghost slot.
3. **Unqualified forms.** Everyone who enquires costs reception time.
4. **No tracking to treatments.** You know cost per enquiry, not cost per treated patient.

## What does the system look like?

- **Clinician-led video:** plain answers to what patients ask before booking, with your expertise, not generated patients.
- **Meta ads and retargeting** to your own pages.
- **Qualification** on the form.
- **Fast follow-up:** instant alert, confirmation text, reminders.
- **Reporting** matched to your records through to attended consultations and treatments.

## What about healthcare advertising rules?

Healthcare advertising is regulated (claims, before-and-after images, testimonials, some treatments) and differs by country, and Meta restricts how some health-related ads can target and describe people. We build a claims check into approval, and you approve every piece before it publishes. Confirm the rules that apply to you.
