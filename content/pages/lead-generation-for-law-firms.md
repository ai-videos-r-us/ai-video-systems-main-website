Lead generation for a law firm works when the ad speaks to a specific situation, the form qualifies, intake responds in minutes and every enquiry is tracked to a signed client, all within your regulator's advertising rules. That's the system we build and run for established firms already spending $5,000 or more a month on ads.

Most firms don't have a traffic problem. They have a gap between the enquiry and the signed engagement.

## Why do law-firm leads disappoint?

1. **Generic ads.** "Experienced solicitors" is wallpaper. A specific situation gets a specific person.
2. **Unqualified forms.** Everyone who enquires costs intake time.
3. **Slow intake.** Across 5.7 million leads, conversion was 8 times higher when the first call came within five minutes ([the data](/blog/speed-to-lead-5-minute-rule)).
4. **No tracking to signed clients.** You know cost per enquiry, not cost per client.

## What does the system look like?

- **Trust-building content** in the firm's real expertise: answers to the questions clients ask before they call.
- **Meta ads and retargeting** to your own pages.
- **Qualification** on the form: matter type, timeline, location.
- **Fast intake:** instant alert, first text, booking link.
- **Reporting** matched to your CRM by person, through to consultation and signed client.

## What about advertising rules?

Advertising of legal services is regulated by your bar or regulator and differs by jurisdiction, including on outcome claims, testimonials and how results are described. Meta also applies its own policies to some sensitive categories. We build a claims check into approval, and you approve everything before it publishes. Confirm the rules that apply to you.
