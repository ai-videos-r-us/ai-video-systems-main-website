The best AI video ads agency for a service business is the one judged on qualified calls and customers, not views, and that keeps your proof real, gets your approval before anything publishes, and connects the video to distribution, follow-up and reporting. Ask the seven questions below. A provider that only sells a number of videos a month is a production shop, not a lead generation partner.

## What are the seven questions?

1. **What do you report?** Views and watch time, or qualified calls and cost per customer? Ask for a sample report.
2. **Where does the material come from?** Your real proof and expertise, or generic scripts?
3. **How do you keep proof real?** They should never generate customers, results or testimonials. See [will AI video ads look fake?](/guides/will-ai-video-ads-look-fake-and-damage-my-brand).
4. **Who approves what goes live?** You should, every time.
5. **Where does the video get distributed, and what happens to the people who watch?** Retargeting and a landing path, or nothing.
6. **Who follows up with the leads?** [Speed decides conversion](/blog/speed-to-lead-5-minute-rule).
7. **What's guaranteed?** Views mean nothing. Read the terms on any refund or guarantee.

## Video shop or lead generation system?

| | Video production shop | Lead generation system with AI video |
|---|---|---|
| Sells | A number of videos | Qualified calls and customers |
| Reports | Views, engagement | Lead, qualified opportunity, sale |
| Distribution | Usually yours | Managed, with retargeting |
| Follow-up | Not in scope | In scope |

## What does volume buy you?

More testing, not more posts. A videographer at $1,000 to $5,000+ per project (published price guides) means a few pieces a year; AI production lets you test many angles and find the ones that attract buyers. See [how to make video ads without filming every week](/guides/video-ads-without-filming-every-week).

## When is an AI video agency the wrong choice?

If you don't have a proven offer, somebody to follow up quickly, or a way to capture the attention, more video just scales the gap. See [when AI video isn't worth it](/blog/is-ai-video-marketing-worth-it).
