Lead generation for a kitchen fitter or remodeller works when it earns trust before the enquiry and moves fast after it: real finished work in the ad, a qualifying step on the form, a call within minutes, and tracking that ends at a signed job. That's what we build and run for established firms already spending $5,000 or more a month on ads.

A kitchen or bathroom job is high-value, considered and trust-heavy. Buyers compare several firms, and the one that shows proof and answers first usually sets the frame everyone else is compared against.

## Why do kitchen leads go to the wrong firm?

Three reasons, all fixable:

1. **No proof before the enquiry.** The buyer can't tell your work from the next firm's.
2. **Slow response.** Across 5.7 million leads, conversion was 8 times higher when the first call came within five minutes, and under 1% of first attempts happen that fast ([the data](/blog/speed-to-lead-5-minute-rule)).
3. **No tracking to signed jobs.** You know what a lead costs, not what a job costs.

## What does the system look like for a kitchen firm?

- **Proof-led video** from your finished kitchens and bathrooms, customer stories, and your answers to the questions buyers ask: price ranges, timelines, disruption, what if something goes wrong.
- **Meta ads and retargeting** to your own pages, so people who watched keep hearing from you.
- **Qualification on the form**, so surveys go to serious buyers.
- **Fast follow-up:** instant alert, first text within a minute, a booking link.
- **Reporting to signed jobs:** every lead matched to your CRM by person, so cost per job is a real number.
