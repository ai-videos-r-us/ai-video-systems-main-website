Video ads for lead generation work when the video is a multiplier on a proven offer, not a substitute for one. An AI-led video campaign for Ironclad Finance produced more than 5 million views and 74 inbound enquiries in seven weeks — a result that came from an established financial-services business with real proof, amplified by content, not manufactured by the content alone.

Most video ad campaigns are judged on views, watch time, and engagement — numbers that feel like progress and produce nothing a sales team can call. An AI video ads agency should be judged on the same standard as any other lead generation channel: enquiries, qualified opportunities, and eventually revenue.

## Why AI video changes what's actually possible

The advantage of AI production isn't cheaper video. It's volume and speed at a quality that used to require a full crew — which matters because fresh creative consistently outperforms tired creative, and most businesses can't afford to shoot often enough to stay fresh. AI production makes continuous testing affordable: new concepts, new angles, new proof points, tested against each other on a cycle a traditional shoot schedule can't match.

## What video is actually good at

Video demonstrates expertise and builds trust in a way static images can't always match — which is why it performs disproportionately well in categories where the buyer needs to believe you before they'll talk to you: financial services, home services, anything consultative or high-trust. For Mortgage Fit, that meant hundreds of thousands of targeted views and 20+ qualified mortgage leads in five weeks, in a category where trust is the entire sale.

None of that happens on video alone. Video's real function in the Attention-to-Revenue System is building the warm audience — the [demand generation half](/blog/demand-generation-vs-lead-generation) of the system — that the paid acquisition and follow-up layer then captures and converts. Views that don't convert to a retargetable, followed-up audience are an Attention Leak, not a result.

## What we actually manage

- **Concept and script development**, built from the buyer's real objections and proof, not generic templates.
- **AI production at volume** — enough variations to test messaging continuously, with brand rules and human approval on every piece before it publishes.
- **Distribution**, paid and organic, to the audiences most likely to become buyers.
- **Retargeting and capture**, so the interest video creates gets a path to an enquiry instead of evaporating.

## When video ads won't produce leads

If there's no proven offer behind the video, no landing path once someone's interested, or no sales capacity to handle more warm demand, more video volume just produces more watched attention with nowhere to go — the same Attention Leak at a bigger scale. Video amplifies what's already working; it doesn't invent an offer that isn't.
